## The rule used in this document

The original extension category is deliberately ignored.

A fashion extension is not treated as fashion software. Its underlying engine is:

- a subject-reference slot
- a transferable overlay or object slot
- a pose or geometry slot
- an environment slot
- identity-preserving regeneration
- image-to-video continuation

A viral-clips extension is not treated as a social-media tool. It is a temporal relevance extractor.

A music generator is not treated as an entertainment product. It is a programmable timing, memory and audio-cue engine.

An avatar generator is not treated as a marketing spokesperson. It is a persistent, consented human-interface layer.

Every idea below had to pass this test:

> Could the product still make sense if the original extension name and industry disappeared?

If the answer was no, the idea was rejected.

# Underlying engine families

1. **Reference-preserving substitution** — replace selected subjects, objects, appearances, poses or environments while preserving source structure.
2. **Multi-reference continuity** — keep a person, asset, site, tool or visual identity consistent across generated scenes.
3. **Counterfactual scene generation** — create explicitly hypothetical alternatives by changing controlled variables.
4. **Spatial grounding** — attach meaning, instructions, questions or workflow triggers to exact image regions.
5. **Temporal synthesis and transformation** — create, continue or transform moving scenes while retaining reference continuity.
6. **Temporal relevance extraction** — find and index meaningful moments in long media.
7. **Realtime multimodal interaction** — combine voice, image and iterative visual change with low delay.
8. **Multi-model adversarial reasoning** — run specialist perspectives independently and preserve disagreement.
9. **Autonomous external-intelligence loops** — search, crawl, detect change and convert findings into approved actions.
10. **Persistent synthetic interfaces** — maintain consented people or characters across changing media and languages.
11. **Governed media localisation** — turn one approved source into controlled language and accessibility variants.
12. **Similarity and provenance analysis** — detect duplication, reuse, derivative patterns and suspicious similarity.
13. **Schema-to-output compilation** — turn typed inputs and rules into documents, diagrams, media and workflows.
14. **Provider-neutral execution** — route work according to capability, cost, privacy, jurisdiction and availability.

# Radical business app ideas

## 1. Titan Privacy Twin

A privacy transformation system for field-service photos and videos. Instead of merely blurring faces, it replaces identifiable customers, workers, children or bystanders with stable synthetic surrogates while preserving pose, body orientation, task sequence, tool interaction and site geometry.

It can also transform distinctive property details into neutral but structurally equivalent environments for training use.

**Engines:** FashionStudio, AIVideoToVideo, AdvancedImage, AiVideoPro and UGCFactory.  
**Buyers:** franchises, insurers, training organisations, NDIS and aged-care contractors, restoration and facilities groups.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–2,999/month plus processing.  
**Boundary:** originals remain immutable; every transformed copy is labelled as a privacy-preserving derivative.

## 2. Titan Expert-in-the-Scene

A technician photographs the real work area. A senior expert records or selects the correct procedure. The system inserts a consistent synthetic representation of the expert into the customer’s actual environment and produces a site-specific demonstration showing where to stand, which component to approach, which tool to use and when to stop.

**Engines:** FashionStudio, UGCCreator, AiVideoPro, CreativeSuiteAnnotations and AiPersona.  
**Buyers:** manufacturers, franchises, HVAC, appliance repair, solar, pools and facilities maintenance.  
**Pricing:** AUD 5–30 per procedure or AUD 499–3,000/month.

## 3. Titan Human Factors Lab

A visual task-design and accessibility simulator. The same real workspace is populated with controlled profiles: short and tall workers, left- and right-handed workers, restricted reach, wheelchair or walker users, workers carrying equipment, two-person teams and vulnerable occupants.

The product surfaces potential reach, clearance, line-of-sight, manual-handling, access, privacy and supervision problems.

**Engines:** FashionStudio, AIPhotoshoot, CreativeSuiteAnnotations and ModelCouncil.  
**Buyers:** facilities designers, occupational-health consultants, disability-access services, training providers and large employers.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–1,499/month or paid assessment packs.  
**Boundary:** planning aid, not an engineering or accessibility certification.

## 4. Titan Counterfactual Incident Theatre

A training system that creates controlled alternatives to a real incident or near miss by changing one variable at a time: worker position, tool placement, obstruction, lighting, PPE state, access path, supervision, sequence timing or emergency response.

Original evidence and synthetic alternatives are always shown separately.

**Engines:** FashionStudio, AdvancedImage, AiVideoPro, AIVideoToVideo and ModelCouncil.  
**Buyers:** insurers, franchises, restoration, safety consultants and facilities operators.  
**Pricing:** AUD 500–5,000 per incident or enterprise subscription.

## 5. Titan RoleSwap Academy

Transforms a real customer interaction into role-reversed simulations. Workers can experience the scene from the perspective of a customer, vulnerable occupant, supervisor, contractor or person with limited English.

**Engines:** FashionStudio, UGCFactory, AiPersona, VideoDubbing and ElevenLabsVoiceChat.  
**Buyers:** home-service franchises, property management and customer-service training organisations.  
**Pricing:** AUD 20–60 per worker/month.

## 6. Titan Visual Work Rehearsal

Creates a pre-job rehearsal using the actual site, actual asset and approved procedure. The worker sees the likely task order, tools, known hazards, evidence points, stopping conditions and escalation rules before starting.

**Engines:** UGCCreator, AiVideoPro, CreativeSuiteAnnotations, AIWriterTemplates and WorkCore.  
**Pricing:** AUD 5–20 per rehearsal or AUD 99–499 per technician/month.

## 7. Titan Service Outcome Contract

A visual agreement that records what the customer expects the completed service to look like. Customers approve exact regions, acceptable outcome classes, exclusions, uncertainty zones and optional upgrades instead of signing ambiguous text.

**Engines:** AdvancedImage, AIRealtimeImage, CreativeSuiteAnnotations and CreativeSuiteAITemplate.  
**Buyers:** cleaning, painting, restoration, landscaping, resurfacing and property preparation.  
**Pricing:** AUD 2–15/job or AUD 99–599/month.

## 8. Titan Remote Hands

An asynchronous remote-assistance system where expert markings become executable instructions. Exact component marks, movement directions, tool positions, sequence numbers and stop conditions compile into a visual sequence, spoken guidance and WorkCore checkpoints.

**Engines:** CreativeSuiteAnnotations, CreativeSuiteAITemplate, AiPresentation and AzureTTS.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–1,999/month plus sessions.

## 9. Titan Visual Exception Compiler

A worker marks something unusual in a photo. The marked region becomes a typed operational exception, such as a variation, hazard, asset defect, customer damage, inaccessible scope, warranty query, compliance incident or supervisor review.

The engine creates the correct form, evidence requirements, approvals and WorkCore actions.

**Engines:** CreativeSuiteAnnotations, AIWriterTemplates, ModelCouncil and WorkCore.  
**Pricing:** AUD 99–699/month.

## 10. Titan Evidence Questioner

An AI that refuses to guess from insufficient evidence. It identifies ambiguity and requests exact additional evidence, such as another angle, the underside, the model plate, a scale reference, better lighting or a short sound recording. Each request includes a visual overlay showing what is needed.

**Engines:** CreativeSuiteAnnotations, ModelCouncil, AIRealtimeImage and OpenAIRealtimeChat.  
**Pricing:** AUD 1–10 per guided survey or AUD 149–799/month.

## 11. Titan Trigger Market

An external-event-to-revenue engine. It watches storms, heatwaves, smoke events, recalls, building approvals, property transactions, new commercial openings, regulation changes, seasonal shifts and public maintenance notices.

Each event is mapped to relevant services, locations, customer segments and approved campaigns, then converted into WorkCore opportunities.

**Engines:** BlogPilot, AIWebChat, Perplexity, AIWriterTemplates and WorkCore CRM.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–2,999/month or attributable-revenue fee.

## 12. Titan Supplier Shock Radar

Continuously detects price rises, discontinued products, shortages, recalls, delays, compatible substitutes and supplier instability. It identifies affected estimates, recurring jobs, stock and customer commitments before disruption becomes an emergency.

**Engines:** BlogPilot, AIWebChat, Perplexity and ModelCouncil.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–1,999/month.

## 13. Titan Warranty Recovery Agent

Scans completed and active jobs for costs recoverable under manufacturer warranty, supplier warranty, recalls, service campaigns, insurance, contracts or subcontractor liability. It gathers evidence and prepares the recovery request.

**Engines:** AIWebChat, Perplexity, AIWriterTemplates, ModelCouncil and AiPresentation.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–999/month plus 5–15% of recovered value.

## 14. Titan Market Gap Harvester

Mines complaints, reviews, tenders, forums, public records and competitor sites for repeated service failures. It converts patterns into new service packages, pricing gaps, suburb opportunities, guarantees, training needs, acquisitions and Titan Sprout verticals.

**Engines:** AIWebChat, Perplexity, BlogPilot, ModelCouncil and AIWriterTemplates.  
**Pricing:** AUD 499–5,000/month or internal strategic infrastructure.

## 15. Titan Margin Tribunal

Every material quote or contract is attacked by independent model roles before issue: labour realist, access pessimist, material-cost analyst, safety reviewer, customer-expectation reviewer, contract reviewer, scheduler, technician advocate, cash-flow reviewer and adversarial buyer.

**Engines:** ModelCouncil, MultiModel and OpenRouter.  
**Pricing:** AUD 149–999/month or per-quote fees.

## 16. Titan Exception Court

A governed AI review board for policy exceptions such as fee waivers, unusual products, preferred-supplier bypass, incomplete evidence, overtime, repair-versus-replace decisions, customer credits and contractor suspension.

Models argue from policy, fairness, safety, commercial impact and precedent. A human decides.

**Engines:** ModelCouncil, AIWebChat and AIWriterTemplates.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–2,999/month.

## 17. Titan Root Cause Jury

Independent specialist models propose and challenge causal explanations for repeated failures using job history, photos, asset data, statements, environmental events, maintenance intervals and previous repairs. The output is a ranked causal graph with disconfirming evidence and next tests.

**Engines:** ModelCouncil, MultiModel, AIWebChat, AiCaptions and WorkCore history.  
**Pricing:** AUD 99–1,000 per investigation.

## 18. Titan Repair-or-Replace Exchange

Evaluates whether an asset should be repaired, replaced, rented, refurbished, cannibalised, redeployed, subcontracted or retired. Independent engines assess cost, downtime, warranty, availability, sustainability, customer impact and recurrence risk. Suppliers can submit live options without controlling the recommendation.

**Engines:** ModelCouncil, AIWebChat, Perplexity, ProductPhotography and WorkCore assets.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–1,999/month plus marketplace fees.

## 19. Titan Spoken Sensor

A technician films a site while speaking naturally. The system treats speech as timestamped sensor data: it isolates voice, aligns statements to video frames, extracts assets and defects, asks follow-up questions and creates structured WorkCore observations.

**Engines:** AIVoiceIsolator, AiCaptions, VideoEditor, CreativeSuiteAnnotations and AIWriterTemplates.  
**Pricing:** AUD 1–10 per inspection or AUD 49–199 per technician/month.

## 20. Titan Machine Ear

A longitudinal acoustic passport for equipment. Recordings from each visit are compared for new vibration, bearing noise, cavitation, fan imbalance, cycling changes, pump strain or intermittent faults.

**Engines:** AIVoiceIsolator, audio infrastructure and a new acoustic-classification layer.  
**Pricing:** AUD 1–10 per asset/month.  
**Gap:** diagnostic acoustic models must be built and validated.

## 21. Titan Invisible Supervisor

A hands-free procedural companion that runs alongside the work. It delivers timed steps, dwell-time cues, mandatory verbal confirmations, evidence reminders, branching questions, stop conditions and supervisor handoff.

**Engines:** ElevenLabsVoiceChat, OpenAIRealtimeChat, AIWriterTemplates and AzureTTS.  
**Pricing:** AUD 20–80 per worker/month.

## 22. Titan Multilingual Site Bridge

A live three-party communication layer between worker, customer and business rules. It translates both directions while preserving approved terminology, prices, warnings, exclusions, uncertainty and transcript provenance.

**Engines:** ElevenLabsVoiceChat, OpenAIRealtimeChat, VideoDubbing, AiCaptions and AzureTTS.  
**Pricing:** AUD 49–149 per worker/month plus minutes.

## 23. Titan Operational Memory Miner

Extracts tacit knowledge from senior workers without asking them to write manuals. Normal work recordings become decision points, exceptions, sensory clues, failure patterns, diagnostic questions, safe shortcuts and escalation triggers in a reviewable decision graph.

**Engines:** AiViralClips, AIVoiceIsolator, AiCaptions, ModelCouncil and AIWriterTemplates.  
**Pricing:** AUD 5,000–50,000 per knowledge-capture program or enterprise subscription.

## 24. Titan Evidence Reuse Detector

Detects reused or suspiciously similar evidence across jobs: duplicate or cropped photos, stock images, repeated report paragraphs, copied incident narratives, reused signatures and old before-and-after images attached to new work.

**Engines:** AIPlagiarism, vision models, captions and a new provenance index.  
**Buyers:** insurers, facilities groups, franchisors, marketplaces and property managers.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–5,000/month.

## 25. Titan Quote Fingerprint

Detects suspicious similarity between supposedly independent quotes or tenders using language, formatting, uncommon errors, pricing structures, line-item order, metadata, image reuse and submission timing. It flags review risk rather than alleging collusion automatically.

**Engines:** AIPlagiarism, ModelCouncil and document analysis.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–1,999/month.

## 26. Titan Asset Identity Graph

Recognises the same physical asset across jobs, changing angles, repaired states, replacement components, serial plates, surface marks and installation contexts.

**Engines:** AIPhotoshoot reference handling, NanoBanana, vision models and ProductPhotography normalisation.  
**Pricing:** AUD 1–10 per asset/month.

## 27. Titan Site-Specific Safety Theatre

Builds generated training environments from the actual customer site and introduces labelled hypothetical conditions such as blocked exits, pets, children, incorrect tools, poor lighting, missing isolation, weather effects and simultaneous contractors.

**Engines:** UGCFactory, UGCCreator, AiVideoPro, FashionStudio and ElevenLabsVoiceChat.  
**Pricing:** AUD 20–60 per worker/month.

## 28. Titan Digital Site Camouflage

Preserves a work process while removing property identity. It changes distinctive décor, personal photographs, addresses, documents, number plates, faces and unique external features while retaining operational geometry and sequence.

**Engines:** AdvancedImage, AIVideoToVideo, FashionStudio and CreativeSuiteAnnotations.  
**Pricing:** AUD 199–1,999/month plus processing.

## 29. Titan Procedure Metronome

An adaptive audio workflow for timing-sensitive tasks. It supplies spoken steps, rhythmic stage changes, dwell-time countdowns, equipment-cycle cues, confirmation tones, warning patterns and exception branching.

**Engines:** AiMusic, AiMusicPro, AzureTTS and ElevenLabsVoiceChat.

## 30. Titan Decision Packet Compiler

A universal engine that turns a complex operational question into a decision-ready package. It selects evidence, questions, model roles, diagrams, options and approval gates for variations, suppliers, asset replacement, complaints, territory entry, renewals and incidents.

**Engines:** AIWriterTemplates, AIImagePro schemas, CreativeSuiteAITemplate, AiPresentation and ModelCouncil.

## 31. Titan Operational Digital Cast

A reusable set of synthetic customers, workers, supervisors, children, pets and occupants used consistently across training and assessment. Each has known behaviour, language, stress response, accessibility needs and scenario history.

**Engines:** AiPersona, UGCFactory, FashionStudio, UGCCreator and VideoDubbing.  
**Pricing:** AUD 299–2,999/month or licensed scenario libraries.

## 32. Titan Worksite Time Machine

Aligns recurring photos, service notes, repairs, replacements, complaints, environmental events, asset sounds and scope changes into a visual chronology. Observed history and hypothetical future states remain visibly separate.

**Engines:** VideoEditor, AIPhotoshoot, AdvancedImage, AiCaptions and the Evidence Engine.

## 33. Titan Synthetic Branch Drill

A whole-business simulator for testing a new branch, service or territory. It generates coordinated scenarios involving customer enquiries, schedule conflicts, supplier failure, worker absence, severe weather, complaints, urgent jobs, cash-flow pressure and regulation changes. **Engines:** UGCFactory, AiPersona, ElevenLabsVoiceChat, BlogPilot patterns, ModelCouncil and WorkCore.

## 34. Titan Visual Memory Search

A worker points a camera at an unfamiliar object or location and asks whether it has been seen before, what happened last time, which technician solved it, which part was used, whether it is the same asset and whether similar conditions caused complaints.

**Engines:** visual embeddings, asset identity, property memory and realtime voice retrieval.  
**Pricing:** AUD 49–149 per technician/month.

## 35. Titan Autonomous Proof Director

A capture director that dynamically tells workers which evidence to collect throughout a job: opening condition, close-up defect, scale reference, model plate, obstruction, mid-process state, completed region, unresolved issue, approval and final wide shot.

**Engines:** OpenAIRealtimeChat, CreativeSuiteAnnotations, ModelCouncil and AIWriterTemplates.  
**Pricing:** AUD 1–10 per job or AUD 99–499/month.

# Required truth and safety boundaries

1. Original evidence remains immutable.
2. Every transformed image or video discloses its synthetic or derivative status.
3. Counterfactual scenes are never represented as factual reconstructions.
4. Similarity detection produces review flags, not automatic fraud accusations.
5. Human-factors visualisations do not replace certified assessments.
6. Diagnostic councils preserve uncertainty and dissent.
7. Synthetic experts and personas require explicit consent and revocation.
8. Realtime field guidance is restricted by approved procedures and escalation rules.
9. Provider outputs are traceable to model, version, prompt, source and time.
10. Generated training is validated against the authoritative SOP before release.

# Conclusion

The hidden opportunity is not AI-generated content. It is the ability to manipulate operational context while preserving selected invariants:

- preserve the site, change the person
- preserve the person, change the task
- preserve the asset, change the environment
- preserve the evidence, generate a privacy-safe derivative
- preserve the procedure, localise the interface
- preserve the decision, challenge it with independent models
- preserve the event history, reveal patterns across time
- preserve the worker’s natural behaviour, compile it into structured knowledge

The strongest new categories are privacy-preserving operational media, site-specific expert telepresence, visual exception-to-workflow compilation, external-event-to-revenue activation, multimodal field observation, evidence integrity, tacit-knowledge extraction and synthetic organisational simulation.
