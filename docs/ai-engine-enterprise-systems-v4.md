## Purpose

V1 identified radically different uses for the underlying engines. V2 combined unrelated engines into new product systems. V3 narrowed those systems into independently sellable micro-apps.

V4 designs enterprise infrastructure for franchises, insurers, manufacturers, facilities groups, property portfolios, contractor networks, acquisition groups, councils and funded-service programs.

A valid enterprise system must:

1. coordinate several organisations, branches or provider groups
2. justify enterprise procurement and implementation
3. preserve data ownership, authority and auditability
4. create cross-portfolio intelligence, network effects or institutional memory
5. integrate with WorkCore rather than create a disconnected operating system
6. support a narrow land-and-expand deployment
7. keep consequential decisions under authorised human control

# 1. Titan Contractor Trust Fabric

## Enterprise problem

Large buyers govern contractors through certificates, self-declarations and periodic audits. Those records rarely show actual evidence quality, scope discipline, exception behaviour or service reliability.

## Product

A portable contractor trust layer that measures several explainable dimensions instead of creating one opaque score:

- verified micro-skills
- service and asset experience
- evidence completeness
- duplicate-evidence risk
- response performance
- approved variation behaviour
- complaint outcomes
- supervision requirements

## Engines

Evidence provenance, similarity detection, SkillProof, scope analysis, complaint chronology, model councils and portfolio outcome analytics.

## Enterprise deployment

Federated tenancy: each buyer owns its records while providers may consent to portable verified credentials and outcome summaries.

## Land and expand

Land with duplicate-photo and evidence-completeness checks for one contractor class. Expand into work eligibility, capacity matching, tendering, insurance and portable contractor passports.

## Governance

No automatic deplatforming. Providers require correction and appeal rights. An anomaly is not a confirmed breach.

# 2. Titan Claims Evidence Clearinghouse

## Enterprise problem

Insurers, contractors, manufacturers and customers repeatedly upload the same evidence into disconnected portals. Transformations and summaries detach claims from original sources.

## Product

A neutral claim graph linking:

- incident
- property and asset identity
- original media
- statements and call segments
- invoices
- prior repairs
- policy or warranty terms
- contradictions
- missing evidence
- human decisions

The clearinghouse produces controlled views for each party without erasing disagreement.

## Engines

Temporal reconstruction, identity continuity, source-grounded policy retrieval, privacy transformation, ModelCouncil and decision-packet compilation.

## Land and expand

Land in restoration claims where chronology and photographic evidence already drive cost. Expand into warranty recovery, repair-network governance, fraud review and pre-loss prevention.

## Governance

Cryptographic manifests, legal holds, jurisdictional retention, synthetic-media labels and human liability decisions are mandatory.

# 3. Titan Property Failure Observatory

## Enterprise problem

Portfolio failures are reviewed individually, so organisations miss combinations of visual change, sound, maintenance history, environment and recurring exceptions that precede damage.

## Product

A cross-property observatory that links the same rooms, surfaces and assets over time and identifies cohorts requiring targeted inspection or maintenance.

## Engines

Longitudinal visual comparison, Noise Passport, AssetSame, property memory, external-event intelligence and multi-model causal challenge.

## Land and expand

Land with one asset class across a controlled portfolio, such as split systems, roof drainage or pool pumps. Expand into capital planning, insurer risk, supplier intelligence and service guarantees.

## Moat

Longitudinal cross-property evidence connecting condition, intervention and outcome.

## Governance

Correlations must remain labelled as correlations until reviewed and validated. Sensitive occupant data should be excluded by default.

# 4. Titan Manufacturer Field Intelligence Loop

## Enterprise problem

## Product

A closed loop:

1. standardise diagnostic field capture
2. link failures to model, serial range and context
3. cluster symptoms and failed repairs
4. challenge causal hypotheses
5. publish approved service bulletins as executable procedures
6. measure first-time-fix improvement

## Engines

Asset identity, Spoken Survey, Root Cause Jury, bulletin monitoring, procedure compilation and multilingual Expert Overlay.

## Land and expand

Land with one product family experiencing repeat failure or no-fault-found claims. Expand into recall detection, parts forecasting, technician certification and proactive campaigns.

## Moat

# Shared enterprise architecture

## Tenant and federation layer

- strict organisation and portfolio tenancy
- federated sharing contracts
- portable verified credentials
- privacy-preserving aggregate analytics
- tenant-controlled retention and export
- regional data residency

## Evidence and provenance plane

- immutable originals
- capture metadata
- perceptual fingerprints
- transformation lineage
- synthetic-media labels
- source-linked claims
- legal holds
- correction and appeal history

## Identity graph

Neutral identities for properties, rooms, assets, issues, people, contractors, skills, products, materials, contracts, evidence and synthetic entities. Probabilistic matches must remain distinct from confirmed identities.

## Executable policy layer

A common schema should represent obligation, scope, prohibited action, evidence, authority, escalation, disclosure, jurisdiction, effective date, version and rollback.

## Council and uncertainty layer

Enterprise councils require independent roles, citations, preserved dissent, evidence sufficiency, human authority and evaluation against real outcomes.

## Provider-neutral capability router

Route models by capability, privacy, region, BYO-key policy, cost, latency, availability, reliability and fallback restrictions.

## Outcome learning

Learning should use governed outcomes such as confirmed resolution, human review, recovered value, contract result, repeat failure, verified competency, margin and audit findings. Model confidence alone is not an outcome.

# Enterprise governance requirements

Titan may organise evidence, apply approved rules and recommend action. It must not independently:

- determine legal liability
- deny public benefits
- revoke livelihoods
- alter insurance coverage
- certify safety
- issue formal qualifications
- declare fraud
- create regulatory obligations
- publish recalls
- impersonate experts

Every consequential recommendation should expose its sources, rule version, disagreements, missing evidence, human confirmations and final authority.

Worker and customer rights require notice, purpose limitation, access, correction, appeal, anti-surveillance controls, data minimisation and retention limits.

Synthetic media must remain separate from original evidence and retain permanent transformation lineage.
