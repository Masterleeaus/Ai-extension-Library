## Purpose

V1 identified radically different uses for the underlying AI engines. V2 combined unrelated engines into larger systems, platforms and marketplaces.

V3 narrows those discoveries into independently sellable micro-apps. Each concept must:

- solve one recurring and expensive workflow
- have one clear buyer and daily user
- produce measurable financial or operational value
- work as a limited first product without rebuilding all of Titan Zero
- connect cleanly to WorkCore records
- preserve evidence, uncertainty and human authority
- provide a credible route to the first ten customers

These are not throwaway utilities. Each micro-app is a commercial wedge into a larger Titan engine, product family or data moat.

# 1. Titan Variation Snap

## Exact market

**Target trades:** Cleaning, painting, landscaping and restoration  
**Buyer:** Owner-manager or operations manager  
**Daily user:** Field worker or supervisor

## Painful workflow

Workers discover extra work on site but either complete it for free or stop while the office manually creates a variation.

## Product

The worker marks the unexpected area in a photo, records a ten-second explanation, and the app produces an annotated variation using approved WorkCore pricing rules. The customer can approve before work continues.

## Underlying engines

- spatial grounding
- voice-to-structured data
- quote and scope comparison
- model adjudication
- schema-to-workflow compilation

## Narrow MVP

Only support customer-requested additions and visibly larger service areas. Pricing must come from existing rate cards; AI cannot invent prices.

## WorkCore integration

Quotes, quote lines, jobs, variations, approvals, evidence and customer communications.

## Proof metric

Recovered variation revenue and reduction in unbilled extra work.

## First ten customers

Recruit five bond-cleaning businesses and five painters that can show recent examples of unbilled additions. Charge for a 30-job pilot and report recovered revenue.

# 2. Titan PhotoGap

## Exact market

**Target trades:** Remote quoting for cleaning, roofing, landscaping, pest and restoration  
**Buyer:** Estimator or sales manager  
**Daily user:** Customer or field assessor

## Painful workflow

Customers send incomplete, badly framed or contradictory photos, forcing a site visit or unreliable quote.

## Product

A no-login guided capture link checks each image immediately and asks for the exact missing angle, scale reference, data plate, boundary or wide shot.

## Underlying engines

- vision grounding
- evidence sufficiency
- synthetic viewpoint illustration
- realtime conversational guidance

## Narrow MVP

One vertical-specific survey schema. Human estimator review remains mandatory before quote creation.

## WorkCore integration

Leads, properties, estimate requests, attachments, unresolved questions and quote confidence.

## Proof metric

Fewer site visits, faster quoting and fewer requotes.

## First ten customers

Recruit ten businesses already quoting from customer photos. Configure one capture schema for their highest-volume service.

**Daily user:** Technician

## Painful workflow

# Shared micro-app foundation

## Evidence object

Every captured item should include:

- source type
- capture time
- job, property and asset context
- original file
- transformation history
- annotations
- confidence
- human confirmation
- visibility
- retention rule

## Guided capture schema

A common schema should define:

- required media
- framing instructions
- minimum quality
- missing-evidence questions
- confirmation authority
- downstream WorkCore field

## Exception schema

Every exception should contain:

- class
- affected scope or asset
- evidence
- urgency
- customer impact
- required approval
- downstream action

## Human authority rules

Every micro-app must state:

- what AI may suggest
- what the worker may confirm
- what the office may approve
- what requires specialist review
- what cannot be automated

---

# Commercial validation process

1. Recruit businesses already experiencing the problem.
2. Record recent examples, direct cost and current workaround.
3. Establish a baseline before introducing the micro-app.
4. Deliver the narrowest version using deterministic rules where safer.
5. Charge during the pilot, even if the price is modest.
6. Measure recovered revenue, saved labour, reduced disputes or reduced risk.
7. Reject products that require constant manual rescue or cannot prove value.
8. Keep WorkCore authoritative; do not create parallel job, customer or asset records.

# Truth and safety boundaries

- Image similarity is not proof of fraud.
- Sound change is not a mechanical diagnosis.
- Visual asset matching requires human confirmation.
- AI cannot create prices outside approved WorkCore rules.
- Synthetic media must never replace original evidence.
- Scope-comprehension checks do not replace legally valid contract processes.
- Recall and warranty outputs must retain authoritative source and date.
- Live translation must preserve numbers, warnings and approved terminology.
- AI assessments cannot issue formal worker qualifications.
- Permit or site sketches are communication aids, not certified drawings.
- Complaint timelines must distinguish observed fact, reported claim and inference.
