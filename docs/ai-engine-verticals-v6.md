# Titan Zero AI Engine Verticals — V6

**Version:** 6.0  
**Date:** 2 August 2026  

## Principle

These are not generic AI assistants renamed for different trades. Each concept is derived from the vertical’s distinctive assets, timing, hazards, recurrence, evidence and customer expectations.

# Cleaning

Cleaning is not only task completion. Profitability depends on how soil, surface, chemistry, dwell time, access, occupant behaviour and customer expectations interact over repeated visits.

## 1. Titan Soil Genome

**Problem:** Businesses estimate labour from room counts while ignoring how different soil–surface combinations respond to products, tools and dwell time.
**App:** Build a learning graph from confirmed job evidence: surface type, soil class, product, agitation, dwell time, passes, labour and final outcome. It predicts scope risk and recommends the next evidence or test patch rather than pretending to know the correct method.

## 2. Titan Contamination Flow Twin

**Problem:** Cross-contamination risk comes from movement, tools and sequence, but ordinary checklists do not show how contamination can travel through a site.
**App:** Create an editable site twin showing zones, tools, worker routes, clean/dirty transitions and evidence points. Generate labelled counterfactual scenarios showing how a changed route or reused tool could spread contamination.

## 3. Titan Residual Outcome Contract

**Problem:** Customers confuse permanent wear, damage or staining with incomplete cleaning.
**App:** Before work, mark likely reversible, uncertain and permanent regions on customer images. Show approved example outcomes, ask comprehension questions and preserve the agreed visual expectation through completion.

## 4. Titan Chemical Memory Vault

**Problem:** Property-specific reactions, prohibited products and successful test patches disappear into worker memory.
**App:** Maintain a surface-level chemistry memory: products tried, dilution, reaction, odour concerns, material changes, occupant sensitivities and approved alternatives. Workers query it by photo or voice before applying chemistry.

# HVAC

HVAC failures are often intermittent and experienced as comfort complaints rather than visible defects. Value comes from correlating rooms, weather, use, sound, airflow and service history over time.

## 1. Titan Comfort Drift Atlas

**Problem:** Repeated hot/cold-room complaints are handled as isolated calls even when they form a pattern.
**App:** Map complaints by room, time, weather, occupancy, settings, asset state and previous interventions. Reveal recurring drift and generate the next evidence-gathering plan.

## 2. Titan No-Fault-Found Lab

**Problem:** Intermittent faults disappear before the technician arrives, causing repeat visits and unproductive parts replacement.
**App:** Guide occupants to capture sound, display state, timing and operating conditions when the fault occurs. Build a case packet that aligns video, audio, settings, weather and technician tests, then lets specialist models challenge competing hypotheses.

## 3. Titan Commissioning Replay

**Problem:** Critical commissioning steps are recorded as a signature rather than reproducible evidence.
**App:** Direct installers through model-specific visual and spoken checkpoints, preserving readings, images, sequence and exceptions. Create a replayable commissioning record for future service and warranty.

## 4. Titan Airflow Topology Mapper

**Problem:** Room comfort is affected by supply, return, doors, obstructions and occupancy, but these relationships are rarely preserved.
**App:** Create an editable topology of supply and return points, room relationships, likely obstructions and occupant constraints. Use it to guide observations and communicate proposed changes.

# Plumbing

Plumbing evidence is distributed across time and space. The visible symptom is often distant from the source, and responsibility may cross plumbing, building, restoration and occupant behaviour.

## 1. Titan Water Story

**Problem:** Leak investigations lose the chronology connecting weather, meter movement, occupant observations, moisture, prior repairs and visible damage.
**App:** Build a source-linked water event story that separates observations from hypotheses and maps possible movement paths across rooms and building elements.

## 2. Titan Drain Behaviour Passport

**Problem:** Slow or intermittent drainage is described subjectively and compared poorly between visits.
**App:** Standardise short recordings of flow, sound, level change and operating context. Compare the same drain over time and attach interventions and outcomes.

## 3. Titan Fixture Failure Collective

**Problem:** Property portfolios repeatedly repair the same fixture models without recognising common failure modes or warranty opportunities.
**App:** Identify fixture models across properties, cluster failure and repair outcomes, monitor bulletins and create evidence-led replacement or recovery cohorts.

## 4. Titan Water Responsibility Boundary

**Problem:** Plumber, restorer, property manager and customer often hold different views of what each party owns after a water event.
**App:** Create a shared visual issue graph separating source control, access damage, drying, reinstatement, pre-existing damage and unresolved responsibility.

# Electrical

Electrical work is high consequence, and the useful AI role is evidence organisation, circuit memory and escalation—not unsupervised diagnosis or safety certification.

## 1. Titan Isolation Proof Graph

**Problem:** Isolation information is recreated repeatedly and evidence of what was identified, tested and authorised is fragmented.
**App:** Maintain a human-confirmed graph of boards, circuits, isolation points, labels, images, test evidence and authorised procedure versions.

## 2. Titan Intermittent Fault Diary

**Problem:** Occupants report flicker, trips and intermittent behaviour without enough context for efficient investigation.
**App:** Guide safe, non-invasive capture of time, affected areas, appliance state, weather and visible board indicators. Create a structured diary for the licensed electrician.

## 3. Titan Load Change Storyboard

**Problem:** Changes in occupancy, equipment and operating schedules are not connected to later complaints or trips.
**App:** Build a visual timeline of installed or removed loads, usage changes, complaints and authorised electrical work. Surface questions and evidence gaps for licensed review.

## 4. Titan Switchboard Memory

**Problem:** Labels, photos, circuit notes and modifications drift across paper records and technician memory.
**App:** Give each board a versioned visual passport with circuit candidates, confirmed labels, modifications, photos, documents and linked assets.

# Restoration

Restoration is fundamentally temporal. Condition, moisture, access, equipment, evidence and causality change across hours and days, while claims depend on preserving that evolution.

## 1. Titan Moisture Migration Chronicle

**Problem:** Individual readings and photos do not reveal how moisture boundaries move through materials and time.
**App:** Align room diagrams, annotated images, readings, weather, equipment placement and interventions into a time-slider showing observed migration and uncertainty.

## 2. Titan Contents Salvage Tribunal

**Problem:** Clean, restore, replace, store and dispose decisions are inconsistent and poorly evidenced.
**App:** Give independent roles—restoration, safety, sentimental value, replacement cost, storage and customer—a structured evidence packet. Preserve disagreement and create an authorised decision record.

## 3. Titan Drying Strategy Sandbox

**Problem:** Teams struggle to explain why equipment layout or sequencing changes and cannot safely learn from alternatives.
**App:** Create explicitly hypothetical visual scenarios comparing equipment placement, access and workflow under documented assumptions, then track which approved real strategy succeeded.

## 4. Titan Cause Chain Ledger

**Problem:** Claims conflate incident trigger, damage mechanism, pre-existing condition and service response.
**App:** Build a causal ledger that separates observed facts, party statements, inferred links, alternative explanations and missing evidence.

# Landscaping

Landscaping products change after delivery. Growth, seasons, shade, soil, access and maintenance debt determine whether the original design remains viable.

## 1. Titan Growth Debt Index

**Problem:** Designs are sold on installation appearance without exposing future pruning, access, root, irrigation and debris burden.
**App:** Estimate a transparent maintenance-debt class from plant selection, spacing, access, growth habit, structures and customer maintenance capacity. Show assumptions rather than false precision.

## 2. Titan Seasonal Outcome Contract

**Problem:** Customers approve a single attractive concept image and are surprised by dormant, immature or maintenance-stage appearances.
**App:** Generate clearly labelled seasonal and maturity scenarios—installation, six months, summer, winter and established state—alongside maintenance assumptions.

## 3. Titan Soil Response Memory

**Problem:** Amendments, drainage work and planting outcomes are repeated without a site-level memory of what helped.
**App:** Record areas, soil observations, amendments, weather, irrigation, plant response and follow-up imagery. Surface prior outcomes before new treatment.

## 4. Titan Encroachment Sentinel

**Problem:** Vegetation gradually blocks access, drainage, signage, solar exposure and maintenance zones.
**App:** Compare recurring site images against protected spatial regions and alert when growth approaches configured boundaries.

# Solar

Solar performance is a system interaction between panels, roof, shade, weather, inverter, usage, grid and maintenance. One photograph or metric rarely explains the outcome.

## 1. Titan Shade Future

**Problem:** Current shade inspections do not communicate how vegetation, seasons or new structures may change exposure.
**App:** Create labelled future shade scenarios from current imagery, vegetation position and seasonal assumptions, then connect them to inspection and maintenance plans.

## 2. Titan Panel Identity and Degradation Passport

**Problem:** Panel replacements, damage, cleaning and warranty events become detached from individual modules or arrays.
**App:** Maintain array-level and panel-level identity candidates, annotated condition history, service events and supporting warranty evidence.

## 3. Titan Roof Interaction Graph

**Problem:** Solar, drainage, roof access, penetrations, cleaning and other trades interact but are documented separately.
**App:** Create one roof graph containing arrays, penetrations, drainage paths, access corridors, fragile zones and linked maintenance responsibilities.

## 4. Titan Performance Drop Tribunal

**Problem:** Performance decline triggers premature cleaning, parts or electrical work without structured comparison of dirt, shade, usage, inverter, grid and measurement issues.
**App:** Independent specialist roles review available evidence, preserve disagreement and request the next safe test or data source.

# Pool Servicing

Pools are dynamic systems. Chemistry, weather, bather load, dosing, circulation, surfaces and equipment interact over time, making isolated readings weak evidence.

## 1. Titan Water Chemistry Story

**Problem:** Technicians and customers see isolated readings but not the sequence connecting weather, use, dosing, equipment and later condition.
**App:** Turn readings, treatments, weather, photos, usage and equipment events into a source-linked timeline with competing explanations and required follow-up.

## 2. Titan Equipment Sound-Flow Passport

**Problem:** Pump, filter and cleaner changes are noticed informally and lost between visits.
**App:** Standardise sound and visible-flow capture under defined operating states, compare with history and alert for meaningful change.

## 3. Titan Stain Cause Council

**Problem:** Surface staining can have multiple causes and incorrect treatment can worsen damage.
**App:** Combine spatial images, chemistry history, material type, weather and treatment history. Independent roles propose causes, disconfirming tests and low-risk next evidence.

## 4. Titan Swimability Window

**Problem:** Customers want to know when a pool is likely ready after treatment, but timing depends on chemistry, circulation, weather and authorised thresholds.
**App:** Calculate a transparent readiness window from approved rules and actual readings, explain dependencies and request a confirming test before release.

# Appliance Repair

Appliance faults are experienced as sequences—noise, display state, temperature, load and cycle stage—not as static images. Parts and warranty decisions depend on precise asset identity.

## 1. Titan Symptom Capture Coach

**Problem:** Customers submit vague fault descriptions and miss the moment the symptom occurs.
**App:** Guide safe capture of display, sound, load, cycle stage and surrounding conditions, then produce a technician-ready symptom packet.

## 2. Titan Cycle Replay

**Problem:** Intermittent cycle failures are reduced to one sentence even though the timing inside the cycle is diagnostically important.
**App:** Index a full or partial customer recording into stages, sounds, display changes and user observations, allowing technicians to jump directly to the anomaly.

## 3. Titan Failure Fingerprint Network

**Problem:** Rare model-specific faults are repeatedly rediscovered across independent repair businesses.
**App:** Pool privacy-safe symptom packets, failed tests, parts used and confirmed outcomes across a vetted network.

## 4. Titan Parts Compatibility Memory

**Problem:** Compatible substitutions and superseded parts are known informally and can create warranty or fit problems.
**App:** Maintain a field-validated graph of original, superseded and substitute parts, installation context, modifications and outcomes.

# Property Management

Property management problems persist across occupants, owners and providers. The central asset is continuity of state, responsibility, access and promises over years.

## 1. Titan Occupancy Transition Twin

**Problem:** Each tenant transition resets knowledge about property condition, responsibilities, access, appliances and unresolved issues.
**App:** Maintain a versioned property state at entry, during occupancy and exit, linking condition, keys, assets, promises, defects and provider actions.

## 2. Titan Recurring Defect Graph

**Problem:** The same underlying issue is reopened as separate tickets under different descriptions.
**App:** Match new reports against property locations, assets, prior symptoms, repairs and complaints, then suggest whether this is recurrence, consequence or unrelated.

## 3. Titan Maintenance Promise Ledger

**Problem:** Owner instructions, lease obligations, provider commitments and tenant expectations diverge.
**App:** Compile promises and obligations into a source-linked ledger with status, evidence, notices, approval and unresolved disagreement.

## 4. Titan Resident Disturbance Planner

**Problem:** Maintenance is scheduled around provider availability rather than resident routines, pets, vulnerability, noise and access constraints.
**App:** Create a privacy-minimised service envelope and coordinate job timing, communication and worker briefing around approved resident constraints.

# Facilities Management

Facilities value is created through dependencies: one asset, contractor, access failure or missing spare can interrupt many services and contracts.

## 1. Titan Service Dependency Graph

**Problem:** Assets, rooms, contracts, trades and business functions are recorded separately, hiding upstream and downstream consequences.
**App:** Build an operational graph showing which services, assets, spaces, contractors and approvals depend on each other.

## 2. Titan Critical Spares Genome

**Problem:** Spare holdings are based on habit or supplier advice rather than failure frequency, lead time and operational consequence.
**App:** Combine asset identity, failure history, repair duration, substitution evidence, supplier shocks and dependency impact to propose reviewable spare cohorts.

## 3. Titan Contract Shadow Cost Map

**Problem:** Contracts appear profitable while access delays, duplicate administration, rework, evidence burden and handoff failures consume margin.
**App:** Map all observed labour and waiting surrounding contract tasks, including work outside formal line items.

## 4. Titan Operational Resilience Rehearsal

**Problem:** Contingency plans are documents rarely tested against real staffing, suppliers, communications and access.
**App:** Run controlled voice-driven simulations of outages, contractor failure, weather, access loss and simultaneous incidents using the facility’s actual dependency graph.

# Aged-Care and Healthcare Contractors

The contractor’s challenge is not clinical decision-making. It is delivering non-clinical work around infection controls, privacy, mobility, sensory needs, care routines and strict escalation boundaries.

## 1. Titan Resident-Safe Service Envelope

**Problem:** Contractors receive fragmented notes about mobility, privacy, infection control, pets, sensory sensitivity and authorised contact paths.
**App:** Compile the minimum necessary non-clinical constraints into a role-specific, expiring worker briefing and spatial site guide.

## 2. Titan Quiet Work Orchestrator

**Problem:** Noisy, odorous or disruptive work collides with meals, medication rounds, rest, therapy and resident routines.
**App:** Compile approved care-facility windows, task disturbance classes, ventilation needs and resident constraints into a coordinated schedule and communication plan.

## 3. Titan Clinical Boundary Router

**Problem:** Contractors observe concerning conditions but may overstep, under-report or send vague messages to the wrong person.
**App:** Let workers mark an observation, then route it through approved non-clinical categories to authorised facility staff without generating a diagnosis.

## 4. Titan Contact-Surface Evidence Matrix

**Problem:** High-touch cleaning and maintenance protocols require zone-specific proof, yet evidence is inconsistent and burdensome.
**App:** Create a facility-specific visual matrix of required surfaces, sequence, approved products, evidence angles and exception rules.

# Safety boundaries

- AI organises evidence and uncertainty; licensed trades retain regulated authority.
- Generated futures and counterfactuals are labelled scenarios, not predictions or evidence.
- Electrical tools never instruct unqualified people to access live equipment.
- Healthcare contractor tools route observations without diagnosis.
- Sound, images and model agreement do not establish technical truth.
- Original evidence stays immutable and every transformation remains traceable.
