**Version:** 5.0  
**Date:** 2 August 2026  
**Repository path:** `workcore/ai-engine-marketplaces-v5.md`

## Core thesis

Titan should not begin with a broad contractor directory. The strongest model is a **captive-demand, evidence-controlled exchange** where WorkCore creates the transaction, Titan verifies supply, and every completed exchange improves matching, trust and operational memory.

Each marketplace below has an explicit transaction, a trust layer, a narrow cold start and a network moat. Raw customer data is not the product.

# Highest-priority marketplaces

## 1. Titan Skill Liquidity Exchange

Match work to demonstrated micro-competencies rather than broad job titles or self-declared trade labels.

- **Transaction:** A job assignment requiring a defined bundle of micro-skills, tools, authority, geography and evidence.
- **Trust:** Human assessor authority, evidence provenance, licence verification, job-specific capability boundaries and outcome-based confidence updates.
- **Cold start:** Begin with one narrow service—such as split-system filter servicing or end-of-lease carpet extraction—inside one existing contractor network.
- **Revenue:** AUD 10–40 per worker/month plus 1–3% of completed marketplace work.
- **Moat:** A portable micro-skill graph tied to real completion quality, not résumé claims.

---

## 3. Titan Decision Procedure Exchange

Allow experts to package and sell governed decision systems rather than static prompts, PDFs or checklists.

- **Transaction:** A versioned decision packet containing inputs, evidence, model roles, rules, authority and WorkCore actions.
- **Trust:** Author identity, scope boundaries, test cases, validation history, customer reviews tied to outcomes and controlled updates.
- **Cold start:** Start with low-risk packets such as quote margin review, evidence completeness and supplier comparison.
- **Revenue:** 15–30% marketplace commission plus enterprise private-catalogue fees.
- **Moat:** A marketplace of executable judgement with measured operational performance.

---

## 6. Titan Circular Material Loop

Match surplus and recovered service materials to nearby internal or external demand before they become waste.

- **Transaction:** A lot of identified material with dimensions, condition, location, provenance and handling rules.
- **Trust:** Human-confirmed material type and condition, source evidence, prohibited-material filters and buyer acceptance checks.
- **Cold start:** Start as an internal exchange for one company and one clean material category before opening external supply.
- **Revenue:** 2–10% transaction fee, pickup fees and shared-savings contracts.
- **Moat:** A local graph of material availability, compatibility, demand and actual reuse outcomes.

---

## 8. Titan Evidence Verification Exchange

Route high-value or disputed job evidence to qualified independent reviewers using a standard evidence packet.

- **Transaction:** An independent evidence-completeness, authenticity or scope-conformance review.
- **Trust:** Reviewer credentialing, conflict checks, blind allocation, source-linked findings and second-review triggers.
- **Cold start:** Begin with one narrow question such as whether required zones were photographed from valid matching angles.
- **Revenue:** AUD 10–250 per review plus 15–25% marketplace commission.
- **Moat:** A reviewer performance graph and standardised evidence-quality dataset.

---

## 10. Titan Asset Intelligence Exchange

Allow asset owners, service businesses and manufacturers to exchange verified model, failure, maintenance and compatibility intelligence.

- **Transaction:** A verified asset identity, failure pattern, service bulletin, repair outcome or compatibility record.
- **Trust:** Source type labels, manufacturer authority levels, technician verification, asset-match confidence and outcome validation.
- **Cold start:** One equipment family with strong manufacturer or distributor sponsorship.
- **Revenue:** Enterprise data subscriptions, per-asset fees and revenue sharing on warranty or parts transactions.
- **Moat:** A cross-company asset graph linking identity, context, failure and successful intervention.

---

## 11. Titan Expert Overlay Exchange

Sell site-specific visual guidance from vetted experts without requiring live travel or long support calls.

- **Transaction:** An annotated step sequence, visual overlay or supervised remote procedure for one site and asset.
- **Trust:** Expert verification, approved procedure sources, case limitations, technician confirmation and resolution outcomes.
- **Cold start:** One distributor routes existing escalations to three invited experts and uses annotated still sequences.
- **Revenue:** AUD 20–300 per case plus 15–25% platform take rate.
- **Moat:** A library of site-specific guidance and expert resolution performance.

---

## 15. Titan Recall Response Exchange

Connect verified recall-affected assets with qualified local service capacity and standard evidence requirements.

- **Transaction:** A completed recall inspection, remedy or verified no-action record.
- **Trust:** Authoritative bulletin, verified asset match, provider authorisation, controlled parts and completion evidence.
- **Cold start:** One real recall campaign with a manufacturer, distributor or portfolio anchor.
- **Revenue:** AUD 5–50 per affected asset, provider transaction fee and enterprise campaign subscription.
- **Moat:** Installed-asset identity plus a measured campaign execution network.

---

## 16. Titan Warranty Recovery Exchange

Match recoverable service costs with specialists who know manufacturer, supplier, insurer and contract recovery pathways.

- **Transaction:** A successfully prepared, submitted or recovered warranty/contract claim.
- **Trust:** Verified specialist outcomes, source-linked claim basis, transparent fees and staged payment on milestones or recovery.
- **Cold start:** One asset family and one known manufacturer warranty process.
- **Revenue:** 5–15% of recovered value plus enterprise subscription.
- **Moat:** Outcome-linked knowledge of evidence and recovery success by asset and counterparty.

---

## 17. Titan Property Turn Exchange

Sell one coordinated vacancy-turn outcome instead of separate cleaning, repair, waste and garden jobs.

- **Transaction:** A property-ready outcome by an agreed date with one evidence and dependency graph.
- **Trust:** Provider micro-skills, shared evidence contract, dependency tracking, owner approval and remedy rules.
- **Cold start:** One property manager, one suburb and three service categories: cleaning, rubbish and minor maintenance.
- **Revenue:** 5–12% orchestration fee or fixed per-turn platform fee.
- **Moat:** A property-turn graph linking initial condition, provider sequence, cost, time and final readiness.

---

## 24. Titan Independent Assessor Exchange

Match businesses with authorised independent assessors for evidence-backed worker skills, job quality and process conformity.

- **Transaction:** A completed assessment against an explicit rubric and evidence set.
- **Trust:** Assessor credentials, blinded allocation where appropriate, rubric versioning, second review and appeal.
- **Cold start:** One micro-skill with ten workers and three approved assessors.
- **Revenue:** 15–25% commission on AUD 20–300 assessments plus enterprise subscriptions.
- **Moat:** A measured assessor-quality and worker-competency graph.

# Marketplace operating rules

- Start with one anchor buyer, one transaction type and curated supply.
- Use concierge matching before marketplace automation.
- Keep trust contextual by skill, service, asset, geography and transaction.
- Record every exchange as a typed WorkCore transaction with evidence and outcome.
- Preserve appeals and correction rights.
- Never convert an anomaly into an automatic fraud finding.
- Keep original evidence immutable and synthetic derivatives labelled.
- Use licensed partners for regulated insurance, guarantee, payment or qualification functions.
- Build network effects from verified credentials, outcomes and compatibility—not the resale of identifiable customer data.
