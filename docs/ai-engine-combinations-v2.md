## Purpose

This pass does not convert individual extensions into adjacent products.

Every concept combines **three to six independent engine families** into a system that no source extension could provide alone. The aim is to create applications that:

- solve expensive operational problems
- generate proprietary data or network effects
- improve through real WorkCore outcomes
- preserve evidence, uncertainty and human authority
- can become standalone apps, platform services or marketplaces

A valid concept must combine unrelated capabilities such as spatial vision, temporal media understanding, realtime voice, external intelligence, model adjudication, synthetic simulation, privacy transformation, evidence provenance, localisation, identity continuity and workflow compilation.

# 1. Titan Service DNA Compiler

## Core proposition

Observe many successful and unsuccessful jobs, then compile the hidden operating system of the business.

## Engine combination

- temporal relevance extraction
- voice isolation and transcription
- spatial grounding
- multi-model adversarial reasoning
- schema-to-workflow compilation
- governed localisation

## Operating loop

The system ingests selected videos, voice notes, photos, quotes, checklists and outcomes. It extracts repeated task sequences, decisions, exceptions, quality thresholds and customer-language patterns. Specialist models challenge each proposed rule and separate universal method from worker preference, site-specific adaptation and unsafe shortcut.

Validated patterns become WorkCore workflows, forms, estimate rules, visual instructions and training. Their effect on margin, quality, speed and complaints is then measured.

## Buyers and model

Franchises, acquisition groups, mature service businesses and Titan Sprout. Charge AUD 10,000–100,000 for a business capture, followed by AUD 499–5,000 per month.

## Defensibility

The moat is a measured service-method graph connecting conditions, decisions and outcomes—not a generic SOP library.

# 2. Titan Procedure Mutation Lab

## Core proposition

Detect when workers depart from an approved procedure and determine whether the deviation is innovation, necessary adaptation or dangerous drift.

## Engine combination

- temporal media extraction
- spatial grounding
- similarity analysis
- multi-model adjudication
- counterfactual scene generation
- procedure compilation

## Operating loop

The app compares field execution with the approved sequence and evidence contract. It clusters recurring deviations and identifies those associated with faster work, lower material use, improved quality or failure.

Promising variations are stress-tested through generated counterexamples and specialist review. Approved changes become controlled trial procedures with versioning, measurement and rollback.

## Buyers and model

Franchises, restoration, commercial cleaning, testing and maintenance networks. AUD 499–5,000 per month.

## Defensibility

It learns the difference between healthy operational evolution and uncontrolled process decay.

# 3. Titan Zero-Day Service Detector

## Core proposition

Discover emerging service demand before competitors have named the problem.

## Engine combination

- autonomous web research
- source-change detection
- model adjudication
- service schema compilation
- synthetic scenario generation
- media assembly

## Operating loop

The system monitors product forums, recalls, weather patterns, regulation changes, complaints and technician discussions. It detects new clusters of failure or customer frustration, then uses independent models to test whether the signal is real, local, actionable and commercially significant.

When a signal passes review, the system drafts the service definition, target customer, price logic, exclusions, workflow, evidence requirements, worker briefing and pilot campaign. WorkCore runs a controlled launch and measures conversion, delivery cost and repeat demand.

## Buyers and model

Titan Sprout, franchises, HVAC, solar, appliance, restoration, pest and property services. AUD 1,000–10,000 per month or performance-linked pricing.

## Defensibility

The value is the closed loop from weak external signal to validated service launch.

# 4. Titan Trust Escrow

## Core proposition

Release payment or marketplace settlement only when scope, evidence, comprehension and exceptions are reconciled.

## Engine combination

- spatial scope grounding
- evidence sufficiency
- provenance and similarity detection
- realtime customer interaction
- model adjudication
- workflow execution

## Operating loop

The accepted visual scope and exclusions are recorded before work. The system directs evidence capture, detects reused or contradictory media, and routes work outside scope through variation approval.

At completion it compares the agreed outcome, evidence and unresolved issues. Payment can be released, partially held or escalated under predefined rules. AI does not decide contested facts; it organises evidence and applies rules agreed by both parties.

## Buyers and model

Service marketplaces, property managers, insurers and franchisors. Charge 0.5–2% of protected transaction value or AUD 299–5,000 per month.

## Defensibility

A transaction trust graph connecting agreement, evidence, decisions, exceptions and outcomes.

# 5. Titan Diagnostic Bounty Network

## Core proposition

Anonymise rare field problems and let vetted human experts and AI councils compete to resolve them.

## Engine combination

- privacy-preserving image and video transformation
- temporal relevance extraction
- model councils
- realtime expert interaction
- evidence provenance
- marketplace workflows

## Operating loop

Customer and property identity are removed while diagnostic evidence is preserved. A concise case packet is routed to matching experts, manufacturers or specialist communities. Independent hypotheses, required tests and confidence are collected.

The council compares contradictions and identifies the next evidence request. Experts are paid when a resolution is validated, and the confirmed pattern is added to WorkCore knowledge.

## Buyers and model

Appliance, HVAC, pool, solar, facilities and equipment-support networks. Subscription plus 10–20% of expert bounties.

## Defensibility

A graph of rare failures, diagnostic paths and measured expert performance.

# 8. Titan Shadow Work Mapper

## Core proposition

Reveal critical work employees perform outside official systems and procedures.

## Engine combination

- narrated field capture
- temporal relevance extraction
- WorkCore event comparison
- similarity and pattern analysis
- model synthesis
- workflow compilation

# Shared platform services required

## 1. Neutral Council Engine

ModelCouncil must be independent of AIChatPro and support typed specialist roles, parallel execution, evidence citations, disagreement preservation, evidence sufficiency and authority boundaries.

## 2. WorkCore Procedure Compiler

AIWriterTemplates and visual schema systems should compile governed procedures into forms, tasks, evidence contracts, approvals, escalation, estimates, customer explanations, training and WorkCore commands.

## 4. Reference and Identity Graph

A neutral layer is needed for people, properties, rooms, assets, tools, materials, products, expected outcomes and synthetic identities.

## 5. Temporal Media Index

A common model should connect transcripts, clips, visual observations, spoken observations, event time, job time, causal hypotheses and evidence links.

## 6. Provider-Neutral Capability Router

Jobs should be routed by capability, privacy, BYO key, jurisdiction, cost, latency, availability, quality history and fallback policy.

## 7. Synthetic Simulation Registry

Every generated scenario should retain source facts, changed variables, assumptions, model version, intended use, prohibited use and validation status.

## 8. Outcome Learning Layer

Every app should feed real outcomes back into confidence, model choice, pricing, procedure versions, marketplace ranking and risk rules.

# Truth and governance boundaries

- Synthetic scenarios are hypotheses or training artefacts, never factual evidence.
- Model agreement is not truth and must not erase dissent.
- Safety, legal, warranty and compliance decisions require authorised human review.
- Workers must not be covertly monitored to build Shadow Work or Service DNA models.
- Original evidence must remain separate from privacy-transformed derivatives.
- Marketplace rankings must not reward confident but unvalidated advice.
- Synthetic customer testing supplements rather than replaces real customer research.
- Dynamic warranty and guarantee products require legal and actuarial review.
- Regulatory text must remain linked to source, jurisdiction, version and date.
- Environmental claims must disclose assumptions and uncertainty.

# Main conclusion

The AI extensions become valuable when their outputs stop being the product.

An image becomes a scope, workflow trigger, privacy-safe derivative, asset identity or decision surface.

A video becomes temporal evidence, tacit knowledge, a process model, simulation or competency record.

A voice agent becomes an examiner, supervisor, negotiation coach, multilingual bridge or capture interface.

A model council becomes a margin tribunal, risk board, root-cause jury, policy review body or marketplace quality layer.

A content automation engine becomes a demand detector, compliance monitor, supplier shock radar or new-service laboratory.

The resulting ecosystem is **operational intelligence compiled into action, tested against reality and improved through outcomes**.
