# Five-Tier AI Merge Report

The cleaning five-tier system was pruned and merged into the chatbot as a chatbot-native AI runtime.

## Retained
- Tier 0 contracts, WorkCore bridge, worker registry, agent factory, memory, skills, personas and model routing.
- Eight Tier 1 managers.
- Fourteen Tier 2 assistants and cleaning vertical specialists.
- Tier 3 atomic execution agents and shared contracts/services.
- Booking, chatbot tool and human-agent inbox modules.
- Provider-neutral messaging, internal/web chat and push contracts.
- Customer tags, reviews, file chat, temporary chat, shared chat and voice isolation.
- A browser-safe autonomous device-agent runtime for offline conversation, knowledge, job, reminder, attachment and sync work.

## Removed from the chatbot package
- Reports, duplicate manifests, tests and packaging tools.
- Deep research and long-running research UI.
- Google, Microsoft, Slack and duplicate WhatsApp connectors.
- Marketing, social automation and campaign agents.
- E-commerce authority and payment/inventory domain code.
- Canvas, blog, content management, highlighting, visual inspection and media-generation studios.
- Provider-specific telephony/voice runtimes and credentials.

## Boundary
Operational records remain authoritative in WorkCore. Device agents may create provisional local actions and durable commands, but server-side agents validate business-wide actions after synchronisation.
